Model. Every planner defines an arrival time over the field; the formation at time t is the front where arrival = t. Drones are spaced evenly along the front, so each drone's swath (altitude) is its share of front length. Several front pieces = the swarm has split.

Peak swath: largest per-drone footprint needed (sets max altitude). Mission: path length of the fastest-moving drone. Speed spread: fastest/slowest drone on the front (1 = everyone flies at the same speed). Chord folds: fraction of MAT steps where consecutive perpendicular chords cross, meaning the rigid formation would sweep backwards. Gold = best in row.
