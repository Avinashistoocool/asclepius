Geometry
Methodology

One model.
Every planner.

  1. 01

    Medial axis backbone

    A Voronoi diagram of the sampled boundary gives the centres of maximal inscribed disks. Spurs are pruned by boundary arc length, and the longest path of the MST becomes the trunk. Its two ends are the start and end caps.

  2. 02

    Arrival-time fields

    Each planner is a scalar field T(x): chord time along the trunk, PCA projection, the harmonic solution ∇²T = 0, or geodesic distance. The swarm formation at time t is the level set T = t.

  3. 03

    Lanes & flight paths

    Fronts are oriented consistently in time and parameterised by arc length s. Drone i owns s ∈ [i/N, (i+1)/N), which partitions the field. It flies the middle of its lane.

  4. 04

    Nadir guarantee

    Each drone's rotated footprint 2h·tan(FOV/2) must reach every cell in its area. The footprints are stamped along every path, and any gap raises the owner's altitude until coverage is 100%.

“Harmonic fronts never fold or cusp, so they hold the lowest altitudes on bent and necked fields. Geodesic and lawnmower fronts keep workloads the most balanced.”
